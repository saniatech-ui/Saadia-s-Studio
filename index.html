About the Studio

Where Every Bride Becomes a Signature

At Saadia's Studio & Salon, artistry meets luxury. Led by Saadia Saif — lovingly known as Saadia Api — our studio in Johar Town, Lahore has become a trusted name for soft, flawless bridal glam and expert hair & skin care.

From your Nikkah to your Walima, we craft looks that feel effortless, elegant and completely you. Every service is delivered with premium products, strict hygiene and a personal touch that keeps 530+ clients coming back.
